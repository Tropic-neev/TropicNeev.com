About Tropic NEEV

More than just clothes.

Tropic NEEV combines social media marketing, e-commerce and affiliate marketing to create a simple way for customers to discover affordable fashion.

We select interesting and trending products, present them through social media and organize them in one easy-to-browse online platform.

Our goal is simple: help everyone find the right fashion within their budget.
