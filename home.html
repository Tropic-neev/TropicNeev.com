About Tropic NEEV

More than just a clothing page.

Tropic NEEV is an affordable fashion discovery platform built around social media marketing, e-commerce and affiliate marketing.

Customers discover products through social media, explore curated fashion picks and can move from discovery to shopping in a simple way.

Our long-term goal is to understand what customers actually want, identify winning products and grow Tropic NEEV into a trusted online fashion brand.
