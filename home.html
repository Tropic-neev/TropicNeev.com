About Tropic

We don't just sell clothes.

Tropic is a student-led fashion startup created as part of the NEEV Startup Stormers project.

Our idea combines social media marketing, e-commerce and affiliate marketing to help customers discover trendy fashion products at affordable prices.

Customers discover products through social media, explore selected products on our website and purchase through trusted platforms.
